Privacy Policy

Family Dashboard is a private application used by a single household.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, it reads Google Calendar events in read-only mode (calendar.readonly). It cannot create, change or delete anything.

How the data is used

Calendar events are shown on a display inside the home. They are kept only on that device, are not sold or shared with anyone, and are not used for advertising or any other purpose.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account under Security, then Third-party apps with account access.
